Explicit evidence labels, consent boundaries and post-interview checks are
editorial synthesis. The guide's broad 7-38-55 formulation and assignments
involving silent video are not used to infer someone's thoughts or truthfulness.
Historical political interviews illustrate questioning rather than current facts.

## Fictional accountability example

A project lead previously committed in a supplied memo to launch on a specified
date. Ask: "Que falta hoy para poder lanzar?" If the reply describes general
progress, follow with: "Que requisito concreto sigue sin cumplirse?" Then ask
who can resolve it and whether the original date still holds, one question at
a time. Do not turn "we are making progress" into a confirmed delivery date.

If the lead cites an unexpected external dependency, explore it before returning
to the essential schedule question. The goal is an attributable explanation,
not winning a confrontation. Verify the memo and distinguish a proposed date
from a commitment the person actually made.
