## Fictional example

A short text moves from separation to tentative connection. Instead of
illustrating each sentence, try one gesture: two surfaces approaching while
retaining a narrow gap. Sketch a frontal view and an oblique view, then fold
two pieces of reused card to inspect the changing shadow and line of sight.

Ask whether viewers perceive connection, threat or closure. If the gap looks
like an accidental construction error, revise its scale or lighting. The model
can test that reading; it cannot establish stability of a full-size suspended
structure. Keep fabrication and audience-access checks distinct.
