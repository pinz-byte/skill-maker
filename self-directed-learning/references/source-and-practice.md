## Fictional learning cycle

Goal: explain a spreadsheet formula and use it correctly on a new dataset.
The learner has three 25-minute sessions this week and can already reference cells.

1. Attempt a small example and mark where understanding stops.
2. Consult one focused explanation about that gap, then rebuild the example
   without copying and explain each part aloud or in writing.
3. Apply it to different data, compare the result with a manual calculation
   and ask a peer or available expert about the unresolved discrepancy.

At review, assess correctness and explanation on the new case, not minutes
watched. If the issue is cell-reference behavior, practise that prerequisite
instead of adding more general videos. This is a proposed schedule, not a
claim that the learner has already demonstrated mastery.
