This is an editorial synthesis. The guide's historical studies and broad claims
about behavior are not treated as current evidence or universal performance
guarantees. The timing guidance is conditional. A discussion map can reveal
patterns of attention, but cannot by itself establish authority or motives.

## Fictional example

A business asks for a dashboard. Discovery reveals that its actual problem is
unassigned customer follow-ups. A dashboard might expose that problem without
solving it. A problem-finding question is: "Que pasa hoy entre detectar un
cliente pendiente y decidir quien lo contacta?"

If the proposed product genuinely assigns ownership and exposes due dates,
a pitch could be: "Podemos mostrarte como quedaria cada seguimiento con un
responsable y una fecha. Revisamos juntos un caso de tu equipo antes de
decidir si esto les sirve?"

Only claim those capabilities if verified. A demonstration tests fit and
comprehension; it does not prove higher revenue. If existing tools can solve
the problem through a simple process change, say so.
