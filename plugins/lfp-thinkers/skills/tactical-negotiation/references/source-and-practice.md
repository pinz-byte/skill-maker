implementation checks and explicit limits on deception are editorial additions.
The guide's broad 7-38-55 communication percentages, neuroscience explanations
and verbosity-based lie-detection claims are not used as validated rules.
Its deliberate mislabeling tactic is adapted into genuinely tentative labels
that can be corrected, rather than feigned misunderstanding.

## Fictional vendor example

A vendor asks for payment within seven days; a customer proposes thirty.
The vendor's stated need is funding materials before work starts. The user's
actual cash constraint and authority must be established before an offer.

- Mirror: "Financiar los materiales?"
- Tentative label: "Parece que el problema no es la fecha final, sino adelantar
  el costo de los materiales. Lo estoy entendiendo bien?"
- Calibrated question: "Que esquema permitiria cubrir eso sin anticipar todo
  el pago del proyecto?"
- Conditional offer, only if authorized and feasible: a documented materials
  deposit with the balance tied to an agreed deliverable.

Check the amounts, delivery criteria, dates and approval authority. Do not
infer that agreement with the summary means acceptance of the payment terms.

For a hypothetical target of 1,000 currency units, the optional buyer-side
ladder gives 650, 850, 950 and 1,000. These numbers illustrate arithmetic, not
appropriate prices or a recommendation to reveal four offers at once.
