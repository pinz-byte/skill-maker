## Decisions to carry into reasoning

**Input integrity.** The factual JSON establishes positions and contacts. The
canon establishes allowed meaning. The selected technical package establishes
what the product writer may discuss. Each has a different job.

**Natal completeness.** Read the actual cusp, ruler and occupants of a house.
An empty house remains interpretable. Element and modality counts use their
declared body set; adding outer planets changes the measure. Do not invent
Quiron, Lilith or other point positions merely because a voice template names them.

**Temporal separation.** Keep natal foundation, personal contact and shared
climate identifiable. In the cascade, solar data does not authorize an additional
selector or duplicate event. Civil-year readings and solar returns are different
products. Residence determines the local period boundary; birth data determines
the natal chart. An exact window comes from calculation, not literary inference.

**Scale and selection.** June's printed-natal transit policy limited bodies and
the number of displayed contacts for that product's shelf life. Do not apply that
limit to every daily or annual reading. Likewise, the machine 3.1 annual scope
does not automatically prove support for all zones and periods. Read the relevant
contract instead of copying historical constants into this skill.

**Technical quality before eloquence.** The old expert-voice demonstration calls
Venus in Aries neither fallen nor in detriment. In Western essential-dignity
terminology Venus in Aries is in detriment. Use this as an example of checking
the rule rather than trusting a confident exemplar. Amorata's client policy in
the canon restricts detriment/fall language; that editorial policy does not
change the technical classification. Do not modify the old skill as part of
using this thinker.

**Two layers, current mechanics.** Preserve human reading and visible technical
support. Under machine 3.1 the engine produces Technique and the model writes
interpretation. Do not resurrect the old prompt that asked an LLM to demonstrate
clinical certainty or independently produce technical conclusions.

**Literary discipline.** Use original, concrete language and coherent development.
The corpus teaches mechanisms; it does not supply new sky facts or the user's
biography. The Spanish client register uses tuteo. Select the current product
language and voice rather than mechanically translating an old English example.

## Calculation reference

[Swiss Ephemeris programming documentation](https://www.astro.com/swisseph/swephprg.htm)
documents positions, speed, sidereal settings, time conversion and house
calculation. Use it to check the calculation setup when needed. Its numerical
capabilities do not validate an interpretation or prove this session ran a check
against JPL Horizons. Never print a precision-verification claim without the
corresponding result.
