## Fictional example

An organization wants to replace its support team with a chatbot because
responses are slow. First establish whether delay comes from triage, missing
information, lack of authority or staffing. Define the gap using comparable
cases and service hours, not a guessed average.

An alternative pilot might automate intake and routing while retaining a
human for exceptions. Specify what existing intake work is removed, who
owns escalation and how staff can correct bad routing. Compare response
time and resolution quality to baseline, while watching reopened cases and
employee workload. If speed improves by prematurely closing unresolved
requests, the pilot has not met its objective.
