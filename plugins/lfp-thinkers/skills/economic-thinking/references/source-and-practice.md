are editorial synthesis. Historical policy claims and forecasts are not current
guidance. The workbook's short phrase about imports/exports must be read with
its subsequent discussion of bond purchases; the skill retains financing rather
than imposing equality on merchandise trade.

## Fictional teaching example

Someone argues that a firm's automation saving means the whole economy gains
the same amount. Define the boundary first. For the firm, costs may fall and
output may change. For the economy, analyze changes in productivity, prices,
workers' incomes, alternative employment and demand. A saved wage bill is not
automatically an equal net social gain or loss.

Compare a plausible no-automation path to the proposed path over the same
period. Identify workers exposed to transition costs and any complementary
jobs. The conclusion depends on adjustment and pass-through, not solely on
the firm's ledger. Do not manufacture elasticities to force a numerical answer.
