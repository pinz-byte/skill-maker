The sequence and scope controls are editorial synthesis, not a fixed Atwood
method. Preferences about process and reader feedback remain optional. The
skill does not copy the author's fiction or ask a model to emulate her style.

## Fictional structural exercise

A courier discovers that a parcel contains a letter they wrote years ago.
Write a short opening in two forms: discovery first, or the earlier writing
of the letter first. Keep the underlying facts constant so the comparison
tests reveal order rather than two different stories.

For each version, identify what the reader knows, what the courier knows and
the question that invites the next page. Choose the version that best serves
the intended experience, then verify that the chosen viewpoint does not leak
knowledge the courier lacks. If the story contains a time-changing device,
establish its limits before relying on it to solve the ending.
