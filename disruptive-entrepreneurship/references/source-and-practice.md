## Fictional example

A founder dislikes unpredictable delivery windows for repairs. Instead of
building a full marketplace, specify the promise: a confirmed arrival window
and notification when it changes. Interview target customers about recent
incidents and their workarounds; do not ask only whether they like the idea.

A bounded test could coordinate a small set of jobs manually with consenting
participants and an explicit time/cost limit. Measure completed jobs inside
the promise, repeat interest and the labor required. If reliability comes only
from costly manual rescue, the operating hypothesis needs revision even when
customers like the service. A better dispatch process might solve the problem
without creating a new company.
